Village Fete Funds Support Local Boxing Gym

Some of the money raised at this year's Village Fete has been used to purchase a Lonsdale Boxing Kit for Spit'n Sawdust Boxing and Fitness Gym.

For the past three years, one third of the money raised at the Village Fete has been used to support a charity or local community activity. This year, Spit'n Sawdust Boxing and Fitness Gym was chosen in recognition of the fantastic work it does within the local community.

The club supports youngsters and adults from a wide range of backgrounds, helping some to build confidence, develop positive relationships and reach their potential.

The club is run by Dave Earle MBE, an inspirational leader who does an amazing job supporting local people and has helped improve the quality of life and ambitions of many youngsters in the area.

Updates to Newspaper service

The Village Store in Bishops Itchington is looking to launch a daily newspaper delivery service for the village. Several residents have already expressed interest, and a few more households are needed to make the service viable.

If you would like national or local newspapers, magazines, or periodicals delivered to your door—either daily or weekly—please get in touch.

The Village Store is a long‑established, family‑run business offering early morning deliveries and serving the local area for over 20 years.
